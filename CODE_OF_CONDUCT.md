# Code of Conduct

All contributors, maintainers, and community participants in `carea` are expected to uphold a respectful, constructive, and lawful collaborative environment.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate clearly, constructively, and respectfully in issues, PRs, and code reviews.
- Respect software ownership, copyright, and responsible-use rules in [`DISCLAIMER.md`](DISCLAIMER.md).
- Accept grounded technical feedback gracefully and back technical claims with reproducible evidence.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, or abusive language.
- Publishing unauthorized credentials, leaked proprietary code, copyrighted third-party binaries/assets, or exploit payloads.
- Bypassing contributor agreements, spoofing upstream maintainers, or misrepresenting `carea` as an official `rea` or `craft-anything` project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, close off-topic or abusive threads, or restrict participation for repeated or severe violations. Report issues privately to the maintainer (**Ahmed Ishtiaque Zarir**) via GitHub.
